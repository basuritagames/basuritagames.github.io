The people, the story and the mission behind Basurita Games.

Basurita Games is an independent game development studio founded in La Plata, Argentina 🇦🇷. Since our founding, we have focused on designing and building original games for web and mobile platforms. Our catalog spans multiple genres, including puzzle, simulation, action and horror, with titles published on Poki and itch.io. Each project is guided by careful game design, distinctive art direction and attention to technical performance. Most of our games are built for the browser, allowing players anywhere in the world to access them instantly, without downloads or installation. We continue to develop new titles with the long-term goal of establishing Basurita Games as a recognized name in independent game development.
